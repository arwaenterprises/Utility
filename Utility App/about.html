Utility by Arwa Enterprises · Last reviewed 1 October 2026

What is Utility?

Utility is a set of simple web tools for warehouse and store teams: scanning items into boxes, sorting boxes, looking up prices, and printing barcode and box labels. It works on phones, tablets and computers, and keeps working when the internet drops.

The tools

  • Box Scanner – scan items into a box, close the box, download the list.
  • Box Segregate – scan a box barcode to see its details; Pallet mode to check boxes against a pallet list.
  • Price Check – scan an item to see its price from your uploaded price list.
  • Year/Season Sort – sort items by year and season into boxes.
  • Item Barcode and Print Box Label – create and print barcode labels.

Who runs it

Utility is built and operated by Arwa Enterprises, serving customers in India and the GCC.
