Utility by Arwa Enterprises · Last reviewed 3 October 2026

What is Utility?

Utility is a set of simple web tools for warehouse and store teams: scanning items into boxes, sorting boxes, looking up prices, and printing barcode and box labels. It works on phones, tablets and computers, and keeps working when the internet drops.
