Privacy Policy

Utility by Arwa Enterprises · Last reviewed 3 October 2026

This is a plain-language summary of how Utility handles data. It is not legal advice.

What we collect

  • Your Google sign-in details: your name and email address, provided by Google when you sign in. You enter your password only on Google's own page; we never see or store it.
  • Your work data: the scans you make (box numbers, barcodes, remarks, quantities, times) and the lists you upload (for example box lists, price lists, item lists).
  • Team details: every Google sign-in is its own team (a team of one), so we keep which team you belong to.
  • If you join a job with a team QR code (no Google account): only the name you type, the job you joined and the scans you make. Your handheld gets an anonymous identity so it can save your work; we do not ask for an email, password or phone number.
  • Simple usage counts: how many boxes were closed or labels printed per day, to understand how the app is used. These counts never contain barcodes or box contents.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show advertisements inside Utility and do not use advertising or tracking cookies in the app.
  • The camera is used only on your device to read barcodes. Photos and video are not recorded or uploaded.

Where it is kept

  • In our cloud database (provided by Supabase) so your work is saved and shared with your team where you use team features. The website itself is hosted by Netlify.
  • On your own device: a copy of your scans and lists, and the app files, so Utility works offline.

How long we keep it

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete your own scans and lists inside the app. The team's administrator can also delete the team's data, including scans made by labourers who joined by QR code. Resetting a handheld only clears that handheld; what was already uploaded stays until the administrator deletes it. A team QR code switches itself off after 3 days without scans, but no data is deleted when that happens. To remove your account or any data you cannot remove yourself, email connect.arwaenterprises@gmail.com.

Who can see your data

You can see your own data. The team's administrator can see and download the team's scans, including the name each labourer typed next to every scan they made. Other teams cannot see it. The app's operator can access the database for support and maintenance.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing us. Laws on personal data differ by country; we aim to respect the rules that apply to our users in India and the GCC.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new review date.
