Your data, your control

  • Only your team sees your dataYour scans and lists are private from other teams. The team admin can see them, and the names of labourers who joined by QR.
  • Your data stays until you delete itDelete it yourself in the app when the work is done, or email us and we will remove it. A QR job with no scans for 30 days is cleared automatically, after a 7-day warning.
  • Never sold, no ads, no trackingWe do not sell data or show ads. Usage statistics are plain counts, never your barcodes.
  • Camera stays on your deviceBarcode scanning happens in your browser. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
